# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young adults in informal groups — roommates, friends, classmates, trip crews, flatmates — who share costs without a fixed occasion. They open the app on a phone between plans, often mid-conversation, to log a bill or check who owes whom. They need the numbers to be obviously correct and the data to feel private to the group.

## Product Purpose

Splitwise Clone helps small groups (up to 5) track shared expenses, balances, and settlements accurately. Success is: anyone in the group can answer “who owes what?” in seconds, without arguing about the math or worrying that balances are wrong.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, private, accurate.

Calm confidence over hype. The interface should feel like a careful ledger — clear amounts, quiet chrome, no theatrics. Privacy shows up as restraint: no noisy marketing chrome inside the app, no oversharing UI patterns.

## Anti-references

- Generic “regular SaaS”: purple gradients, pill-heavy dashboards, identical icon+title+blurb card grids, startup landing tropes inside the product
- Neon fintech / crypto dashboards
- Dense spreadsheet UIs that bury balances
- Playful sticker / emoji-heavy consumer apps that undermine money seriousness

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first** — Amounts and who-owes-whom are the hero of every relevant screen; decoration never competes with balances.
2. **Earn trust through clarity** — One visual system for money states (owe / owed / settled); no ambiguous colors or clever metaphors.
3. **Private by default** — Quiet surfaces, tight information density for the group’s data, no performative marketing chrome in authenticated views.
4. **Familiar affordances** — Standard buttons, forms, and navigation; invent nothing when a known pattern works.
5. **Mobile-ready task flow** — Primary actions reachable quickly; lists and forms readable on a phone in one hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for body and UI text
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Don’t rely on color alone for owe vs owed (pair with labels)
- Touch targets ≥ 44px where possible; focus rings always visible
